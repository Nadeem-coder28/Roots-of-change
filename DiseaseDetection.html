    Tips for a good result

    • Photograph a single leaf, filling most of the picture.
    • Use a plain background and good daylight.
    • Make sure the leaf is in focus.

    About the AI model

    The detector is a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on the PlantVillage dataset of 54,305 leaf images. It recognises 38 classes of healthy and diseased leaves from 14 crops: apple, blueberry, cherry, corn (maize), grape, orange, peach, bell pepper, potato, raspberry, soybean, squash, strawberry and tomato.

    Two CNNs were trained and compared: a custom CNN built from scratch and MobileNetV2 fine-tuned with transfer learning. The website uses MobileNetV2, which reached 99.74% accuracy on 5,430 test images it had never seen during training.

    The model runs entirely in your browser, so your photos are never uploaded to a server.

    The result is an estimate and does not replace advice from an agricultural expert. The model was trained on photos of single leaves taken under controlled conditions, so it may be less accurate for field photos or crops it was not trained on.
